### 🧹 [Clean Desktop](https://cleanmacdesktop.com): macOS menu bar app

Clears your Mac desktop into a dated archive folder without deleting anything. Free, no trial, no license key.

- One-click clean from the menu bar, or automatic daily or weekly runs (catches up on wake if the Mac was asleep)
- Files into month or day folders; name collisions get renamed, never overwritten
- One-click undo for any clean, with a log of the last 60
- Finder-tagged files stay put, and recent files can be kept for 1, 3, or 7 days
- Universal build for Apple silicon and Intel, 1.3 MB, signed and notarized for direct download
- No account, no ads, no analytics

**Tech:** Swift · SwiftUI · macOS

### 🖼️ [Just Frame](https://davepoortvliet.com/just-frame/): iOS app

Turns an idle iPad into a photo frame that shows the date and place each photo was taken, read from the GPS data your photos have been carrying all along.

- Full-screen slideshow from your whole library or any album, including smart albums like Favorites
- Shuffles with no repeats until every photo has been shown
- Reverse-geocodes each photo's location, with a local cache
- Set the hours it runs; outside them the screen goes dark
- $1.99 once. No subscription, no in-app purchases, no analytics SDK

**Tech:** Swift · SwiftUI · PhotoKit · CoreLocation

📲 [Just Frame on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/just-frame/id6807210970)

### 🚗 [Drive AQI](https://driveaqi.com): iOS app

Live air quality on your car's dashboard. The first app to put the Air Quality Index on the CarPlay Dashboard, using Live Activities. No extra hardware, no setup; it just rides along while you drive.

- Live US EPA AQI for your current location, color-coded from Good to Hazardous
- CarPlay Dashboard card, plus Lock Screen, Dynamic Island, and Apple Watch Smart Stack
- Updates automatically as you drive, with PM2.5, PM10, and ozone detail
- Private by design: no accounts, no ads, no tracking. Location is used only to fetch air quality

**Tech:** Swift · SwiftUI · ActivityKit · WidgetKit · CoreLocation

📲 [Drive AQI on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/drive-aqi/id6795573789?itscg=30200&itsct=apps_box_link&mttnsubad=6795573789)
