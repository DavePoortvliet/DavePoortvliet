🚗 [Drive AQI](https://apple.co/3UvORM8) — iOS App

Live air quality, right on your car's dashboard. The first app to put the Air Quality Index on the CarPlay Dashboard, using iOS 26 Live Activities — no extra hardware, no setup, it just rides along while you drive.

Live US EPA AQI for your current location, color-coded from Good to Hazardous
CarPlay Dashboard card via Live Activities (also Lock Screen, Dynamic Island, and Apple Watch Smart Stack)
Updates automatically as you drive
PM2.5, PM10, and ozone detail
Private by design: no accounts, no ads, no tracking — location is used only to fetch air quality

Tech: Swift · SwiftUI · ActivityKit · WidgetKit · CoreLocation

📲 Find it in the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/drive-aqi/id6795573789?itscg=30200&itsct=apps_box_link&mttnsubad=6795573789)
